About
Worry Shredder is a simple therapeutic tool based on externalization and release — techniques used in CBT and ACT. Writing worries down and physically "destroying" them can reduce rumination and provide a sense of closure.

Not a replacement for professional care. If stress is persistent, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.
