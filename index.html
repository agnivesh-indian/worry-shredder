Worry Shredder

A simple tool to externalize & release

📖 About
Worry Shredder is a therapeutic tool rooted in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT). It uses the principles of externalization and release — writing your worries down and physically "destroying" them can reduce rumination and provide a genuine sense of closure.
🎯 How to use
1
Write your worry. Type whatever is weighing on your mind into the input box.
2
Add the note. Tap Add note — your worry appears as a pinned card.
3
Drag it into the shredder. Watch, feel, and hear it get destroyed in a satisfying 2-second sequence.
4
Breathe. Repeat. Track your streak and release as many worries as you need.
Not a replacement for professional care. If stress becomes persistent or overwhelming, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.
